FAQ

I don’t see any badges in my feed

Badges appear next to authors who have posted a token contract or cashtag that KoL'SScan has seen. Scroll a feed with such posts, or open a KOL’s profile and press ◆ Scan profile. After updating the extension, reload the x.com tab.

The card says “calculating…”

Prices come from the free GeckoTerminal API, which allows about one call every few seconds. Keep the card open or come back in a minute.

A call shows “>180d”

The free price API has no history older than 180 days, so very old calls can’t be rated.

What does “deleted” mean?

The post with that call no longer exists on X. KoL'SScan checks this through X’s public embed endpoint and keeps the call in the author’s stats.

How do I switch the language or hide the mascot?

Click the KoL'SScan icon in the Chrome toolbar: there is an EN / RU switch and a toggle for Vik the mascot.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser. See the privacy policy. The Clear button in the popup erases everything.
